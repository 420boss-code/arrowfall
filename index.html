Revision notes

Order of operations

BIDMAS: Brackets, Indices, Division and Multiplication (left to right), then Addition and Subtraction (left to right). So 2 + 3 × 4 = 2 + 12 = 14.

Adding fractions

Find a common denominator first. 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12. Simplify by dividing top and bottom by their highest common factor.

Percentages

"Per cent" means "out of 100". To find 15% of 240, work out 10% (24) and 5% (12), then add: 36. To increase by 15%, multiply by 1.15.

Negative numbers

Subtracting a negative is the same as adding: 5 − (−3) = 8. Multiplying or dividing two negatives gives a positive.

Solving equations

Do the same to both sides until the letter is on its own. 3x + 4 = 19: subtract 4 (3x = 15), then divide by 3 (x = 5). Check by substituting back.

Expanding brackets

Multiply everything inside by the term outside: 3(x − 2) = 3x − 6. For two brackets, multiply each term by each term: (x + 2)(x + 5) = x² + 7x + 10.

Rounding

Look at the next digit: 5 or more rounds up. 3.462 to 1 decimal place is 3.5; 48 260 to 2 significant figures is 48 000. Significant figures start at the first non-zero digit.

Standard form

A × 10ⁿ with 1 ≤ A < 10. 34 000 = 3.4 × 10⁴ and 0.0052 = 5.2 × 10⁻³. To multiply, multiply the A parts and add the powers.

Area

Rectangle: length × width. Triangle: ½ × base × height. Parallelogram: base × height. Trapezium: ½(a + b)h. Circle: πr². Area is measured in square units, such as cm².

Circles

Circumference = πd = 2πr. Area = πr². With r = 5 cm: circumference ≈ 31.4 cm and area ≈ 78.5 cm². The diameter is twice the radius.

Probability

Probability = favourable outcomes ÷ possible outcomes, from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain). The probabilities of all possible outcomes add up to 1, so P(not A) = 1 − P(A).

Averages

Mean: total ÷ how many. Median: the middle value in order. Mode: the most common. Range: largest − smallest. See the Statistics tool above.

About Numberwell

Numberwell is a free maths helper for Key Stage 3 and GCSE-level topics: a step-by-step solver for arithmetic and linear equations, quick practice questions, printable worksheets with answer keys, interactive tools and short revision notes.

The solver keeps fractions exact and shows each step, so you can compare it with your own working rather than just copying an answer.
